# Roadmap

The canonical status and acceptance evidence live in
[`docs/implementation/README.md`](docs/implementation/README.md).

1. Repository governance and quality baseline.
2. Spice-native annotation and composition proof.
3. Deterministic single-agent kernel.
4. Provider, coding-tool, daemon, and TUI vertical slice.
5. Process-isolated runtime plugins and developer loop.
6. Signed architecture-proof preview.
7. Contract stress prototypes.
8. Pre-1.0 stabilization and optional ecosystem breadth.

Phase 8 now enforces the written pre-v1 Go API, durable-state, engine/plugin
protocol, generated-source, and security-exception policy. The immutable
Toolchain preview2 generator contract is released and every owned Agent target
is migrated to schema 6. All three clean-room public-authoring and
generated-source exercises are released. The preview5/preview6
released-generation matrix is hosted-green on Linux and Windows, closing its
two enumerated protocol blockers. This does not freeze the APIs, declare v1, or
claim a released-binary asset matrix.
Stable kernel benchmark budgets and their change policy are mandatory in the
full verifier.
